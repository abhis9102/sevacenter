Design system · v0

A warm, trustworthy identity for seva

The brand draws from the temple itself — the saffron of the marigold garland, the deep red of sindoor and sanctum walls, the gold of the lamp. It pairs that warmth with the restraint a platform handling devotee records, donations and 80G receipts needs to feel credible. Two surfaces share the system: an efficient admin console for trust staff, and a welcoming public page for donors.
